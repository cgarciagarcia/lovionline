Lo Vi Online Studio

The in-house publishing tool of Lo Vi Online, a TikTok account that shares home decor ideas and product finds from MercadoLibre Argentina.

What it does

  • Lets the Lo Vi Online team sign in with TikTok (Login Kit).
  • Uploads the photo posts and videos we create to our own TikTok account, either as drafts to finish in the TikTok app or as direct posts (Content Posting API).
  • Reads the basic statistics of our own published videos so we can learn which content works best.

Who can use it

Lo Vi Online Studio is a private desktop tool. It is only used by the Lo Vi Online team to manage its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.
