Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Summary

Lo Vi Online Studio is a private desktop tool used only by the Lo Vi Online team to publish content to, and read statistics from, its own TikTok account. We do not collect data from the public, we do not sell data, and we do not share data with third parties.

2. Data we access

When the account owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, we receive only the data allowed by the scopes they approve:

  • user.info.basic: open ID, display name and avatar, to confirm which account is connected.
  • user.info.stats: follower, following, likes and video counts of the connected account.
  • video.list: the list of the connected account's own public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts.
  • video.upload and video.publish: permission to send our own photos and videos to the connected account, as drafts or direct posts.

We also store the access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, which are required to make these requests.

3. How we use it

  • To publish the content we create to our own TikTok account.
  • To measure how our own posts perform and improve future content.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the Lo Vi Online team's own computer, in local files that are not published or synced to public services. They are not transferred to any server operated by us or by third parties, other than the requests sent directly to TikTok's API.

5. Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while they are useful for our content analysis. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Settings and privacy › Security and permissions › Apps and services); after that, the stored tokens stop working and we delete them. You can also ask us to delete any stored data by writing to the contact address below.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and is not used to collect data from anyone other than the Lo Vi Online team's own account.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
